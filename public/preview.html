AI-B Trading Desk

Personal AI-Assisted Trading Analysis & Pattern Recognition

What This Is

A self-hosted, single-user trading desk that combines:

  • Chart analysis (upload 5 timeframes → AI reads structure & calls trades)
  • Pattern library (log past setups, AI extracts the data)
  • Risk calculator (deterministic math, no guessing on sizing)
  • Trading journal (track every read + outcome, measure actual performance)
  • AI coach (review your closed trades, evidence-based feedback)
  • Conversational overlay (Franky Lamps — same data, better vibe)

Not included: Live broker feeds, auto-execution, backtesting, or anything that needs your real account credentials. This is decision support only.

📊 Analyze Chart

Upload up to 5 timeframes (5M entry required, 15M/1H/4H/Daily for context). AI reads structure, calls bias, identifies trade setups, flags opposing cases. Higher timeframes set the bias, lower refine timing.

🔍 Batch Scanner

Upload several charts at once (up to 8). Each gets ranked by AI confidence. Use for current scans or building historical reference data in your Pattern Library.

📈 Pattern Library

Log past setups with outcomes. Upload a result screenshot → AI extracts setup/outcome. View recurring conditions in wins vs losses. Qualitative reference for the desk.

🧮 Risk Calculator

Deterministic position sizing. Enter entry, stop, balance, risk% → get lot size, stop distance, R:R ratio. Supports 20+ symbol presets (forex, indices, commodities, crypto).

📔 Trading Journal

Save every analysis. Mark outcomes (win/loss/breakeven), log result in R multiples, tag session (Asia/London/NY/Overlap). Export CSV. All stored on your server.

🏆 AI Coach

Review all your closed trades at once. AI extracts strengths, mistakes, recurring patterns, and one actionable focus for next week — grounded in your actual journal, not generic advice.

Dashboard View

Single-page app. No loading bars, no waiting. Settings sync across the desk — change your balance once, it propagates to the risk calculator and AI reads automatically.

What Makes This Different

  • Honest about what it is: Decision support. No system guarantees profit. The AI reads charts and logs data — you make the final call.
  • Owned by you: Runs on your server (Railway, home machine, VPS — your choice). No cloud vendor lock-in, no third-party dashboard.
  • Integrated, not bolted together: Every feature (Coach, Franky, Pattern Insights) reads from the same journal, settings, and library — one unified desk, not separate tools.
  • Validation layer: AI output is checked for internal consistency before it reaches your journal. If numbers don't make sense, the trade idea is withheld and flagged.
  • Qualitative, not statistical: Pattern library is a memory aid, not a backtester. Small sample sizes aren't reliable edges — but they're worth watching.
